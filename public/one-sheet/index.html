Craig Dube

Fractional product leader for small software companies

I've spent 35 years in software, from developer to Senior Director of Product Management at impact.com and Meltwater. I now work with a few small software companies at a time, one or two days a week, as the product leader you aren't ready to hire full time. Founders call me when the backlog keeps growing, when they're still the product manager themselves, when they want a straight answer on AI, or when they're buying a company and need to know if its product fits.

What I do

  • Roadmap and priorities. I turn the goals in the founder's head into a 12 to 18 month roadmap, broken into sprints the engineers can work from.
  • Customer-first decisions. I use Jobs To Be Done. I start from the outcome the customer wants and work back to the feature.
  • Product manager mentorship. I've hired and coached product managers for years, including several who came from engineering or had never owned a product.
  • AI I've used myself. At impact.com I used AI to review proposed projects. I still build with Claude Code, including my own AI agent, Wren, so I can talk with your engineers in their own terms.

How I work

  • I meet each person on the team weekly, and I don't micromanage.
  • I take the time to learn how your product is built, so I can weigh the tradeoffs with your engineers.
  • I raise problems with leaders early, including the hard conversations.
  • If a full-time product leader would serve you better, I'll tell you and help you hire one.

Week one

I meet the founders and the team and read what's already written down. At the end of the week you get a short readout naming the three biggest problems and what I'd do about them in the first 30 days.

Who I work with

Software companies of 50 people or fewer. My most recent work is in affiliate/partner marketing and media intelligence.
